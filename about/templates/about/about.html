Mikhail Tal – About

Mikhail Tal was a chess player and the eighth World Chess Champion born in Riga, Latvia. He is best known for sacrificing material in exchange for positional benefit and writing detailed books discussing chess strategy. On June 28 1992, Tal died due to liver complications at the age of 55.
